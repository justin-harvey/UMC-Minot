About Our Church

Serving the Minot community for over 150 years

Our Story

The United Methodist Church of Minot has deep roots in our rural Maine community, dating back to 1835 when it was originally established as the Minot Corner Methodist Church on Old Woodman Hill Road. This initial structure was later dismantled and its materials were used in the construction of our present church building, which continues to hold weekly services today.

The deed for the land on which our current church stands is dated 1859, with the land purchased for seventy-five dollars. Our church's history is intertwined with the town of Minot itself, which was incorporated on February 18, 1802, from a portion of the town of Poland. The town was named after George Richards Minot, a historian and judge who aided in its incorporation.

The area's religious history runs deep, with the first meetinghouse in the region raised in 1794 on what is now Death Valley Road. The first church at Minot Corner was built in 1835, reflecting the community's early development as an industrial center with mills and other businesses. With a population of 2,766, Minot remains a close-knit community where neighbors know neighbors and faith runs deep.

We believe in the United Methodist tradition of "Open Hearts, Open Minds, Open Doors" - welcoming all people regardless of where they are in their faith journey. Our church is a place where questions are encouraged, doubts are embraced, and love is unconditional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about worship, location, and visiting us

What time is Sunday service at United Methodist Church of Minot?

Sunday worship is at 10:00 AM and runs until about 11:15 AM at 142 Minot Ave, Minot, Maine. Coffee and fellowship follow the service.

Where is the church located?

We are at 142 Minot Ave, Minot, ME 04258. The building has an accessible entrance. You can reach us at (207) 783-9097 or minotumc@gmail.com.

Is everyone welcome?

Yes. As a United Methodist congregation we live by Open Hearts, Open Minds, Open Doors — welcoming all people wherever they are on their faith journey. Children are welcome, and no prior church experience is needed.

What should I expect at a worship service?

Expect a warm, welcoming community with a mix of traditional and contemporary music, meaningful prayer, and a short message. Come as you are — casual dress is fine, and coffee and fellowship follow the service.

How long has the church been in Minot, Maine?

Methodist worship in Minot dates to the first church built at Minot Corner in 1835. The current church building traces to an 1859 land deed and continues to hold weekly services today.
