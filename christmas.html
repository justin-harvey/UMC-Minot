Advent & Christmas 2026

Christmas at Minot UMC

Whether you join us every Sunday or are looking us up for the first time, there is a place for you at our Christmas services. All are welcome.

Our Christmas Services

Here is everything you need to plan your visit. All services are held at our church at 142 Minot Ave, Minot, Maine, and all are free and open to everyone.

Most attended service of the year

Christmas Eve Worship

Thursday, December 24, 2026

6:00 PM

  • 142 Minot Ave, Minot, ME — accessible entrance
  • Family-friendly — children are warmly welcome
  • A single, in-person service for members and first-time guests alike

Gather with us to welcome Christmas with carols, Scripture, and a warm, come-as-you-are spirit. If you have been away from church for a while, or have never been, this is a wonderful night to come home.

Blue Christmas Service

Monday, December 21, 2026

5:00 PM

  • 142 Minot Ave, Minot, ME — accessible entrance
  • A quiet, reflective service — open to all

What is a Blue Christmas service? Sometimes called a Longest Night service, it is a quieter, gentler gathering for anyone who finds the holidays hard, whether through grief, loss, illness, loneliness, or change. It makes room for honesty, comfort, and hope. You are welcome exactly as you are.

Sundays in Advent

Sundays, beginning November 29, 2026

10:00 AM

  • 142 Minot Ave, Minot, ME — accessible entrance
  • Children welcome

Our regular Sunday worship continues all through the Advent season at 10:00 AM. Come journey with us through the weeks leading up to Christmas.

What to Expect When You Visit

If this is your first time, here are answers to the questions guests ask most. We would rather you feel at home than wonder.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Most people dress casually or in whatever feels comfortable for you. There is no dress code.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. The church has a wheelchair-accessible entrance, so everyone can join us comfortably.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely. Christmas Eve is a family-friendly service, and children are welcome in worship. No need to worry about the usual wiggles.

Where is it, and when should I arrive?

We are at 142 Minot Ave, Minot, ME 04258. Arriving a few minutes early gives you time to find a seat and settle in. Get directions →

Do I need to be a member?

Not at all. Our doors are open to everyone, wherever you are on your faith journey. First-time guests are our honored guests.

Have another question?

We are glad to help. Call us at (207) 783-9097 or email minotumc@gmail.com and we will get right back to you.

Planning to Join Us?

We would love to know you are coming, and we would be glad to say hello and answer any questions. Share as much or as little as you like. This is optional, and everyone is welcome whether you let us know ahead of time or simply walk in.

We Have Been Expecting You

However you spend this season, you have a seat with us. We hope to see you at Christmas, and all year long.
